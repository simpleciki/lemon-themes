# Prompts behind the lemon-bear drawings

Each drawing was made with Codex `image_gen` (transparent background), on a ChatGPT plan.

## Style reference prompt

The look every drawing follows. Give it, with the chosen design sheet, when drawing anything new for this theme.

> Soft storybook coloured-pencil illustration in sticker form: thin, soft warm-brown pencil outlines; gentle grainy pencil shading; pastel palette of honey tan, lemon yellow, sage green, cream and blush pink; a thin white sticker border around every item; no hard black lines. The mascot is a chunky, round honey bear whose head and body melt into one soft shape with almost no neck: a big round head with small round ears on top, small upright-oval black eyes with a tiny white highlight set close together just above a light tan oval muzzle, a small dark nose, faint pink cheeks, short stubby arms and legs, and a body about as wide as the head, never wider. It wears a yellow beret with a small pink heart, a white daisy by one ear, sage-green overalls with a yellow pocket and cream sleeves with tiny yellow flowers.

Draw SEPARATE animation parts in a 3x2 grid with wide empty gaps, nothing touching, all at the same scale: Part 1: the HEAD ONLY with ears, beret, daisy, OPEN dot eyes, muzzle, nose and cheeks, cut at the chin. Part 2: a small patch of fur showing the two eyes CLOSED as gentle curved lines, exactly the size and spacing of the eye area of part 1, nothing else. Part 3: the BODY WITHOUT HEAD, sitting, hugging a lemon, with a rounded patch of fur on top that continues up behind where the head will sit. Part 4: the whole bear waving one paw. Part 5: the whole bear celebrating, jumping with both arms up holding a lemon high. Part 6: the whole bear asleep curled up. No background colour, no white background, no shadows, no floor, no text, no labels, no panel borders.
